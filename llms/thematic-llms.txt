# Thematic - Free Music for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram & Podcasts

> Thematic is a music licensing platform connecting 1M+ independent content creators with copyright-safe music from emerging artists. Free to use: creators get a permanent license at no cost in exchange for including a promotional link in their video description, which activates Content ID whitelisting and prevents copyright claims. Premium ($9.99/mo) and Pro ($24.99/mo) plans available. Co-founded with YouTube pioneer Michelle Phan. Serving creators across YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and podcasts.
